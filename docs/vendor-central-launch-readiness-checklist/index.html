How to use this

A simple GO / NO-GO review before launch.

Assign one owner, one due date and one success metric to every open item. If a critical item has no clear owner or evidence, the launch is not ready yet. Requirements vary by category, region and vendor agreement.

1. Verify

Check the commercial, catalog and operational facts in Vendor Central and connected internal systems.

2. Assign

Give every gap a named owner, completion date and documented source of truth.

3. Gate the launch

Proceed only when the launch has buyability, supply-chain readiness and a first-30-day operating plan.

The checklist

Seven checks before you call a launch ready.

01 · Commercial setup and identifiers

Confirm the vendor agreement, terms, regions, currencies, remit-to details, legal entity, item identifiers and brand ownership. Ensure the same identifiers are used consistently across PO, catalog and internal systems.

02 · Detail pages and variation accuracy

Review titles, bullets, images, A+ content where applicable, browse classification, variations, compliance attributes and retail copy. Shoppers must be able to understand, find and buy the item correctly.

03 · Buyability and inventory readiness

Validate in-stock status, landed availability, replenishment logic, lead times and allocation rules. Confirm inventory can support the launch plan—not only the first purchase order.

04 · Purchase-order and shipment workflow

Map the workflow from PO receipt through acknowledgement, routing, shipment, ASN, ARN, receiving and invoice. Check who owns every handoff and what evidence is retained when an exception occurs.

05 · Freight, terms and deduction-risk controls

Confirm freight terms, routing instructions, carton and label requirements, delivery windows and receiving evidence. Test controls against shortage, compliance and invoice deductions before volume increases.

06 · First 30 days: performance ownership

Set a weekly review covering sales, availability, PO acceptance, fill rate, shipment exceptions, receiving status, deductions, content gaps and retail actions.

07 · Evidence, escalation and GO / NO-GO

Document the validation, owner and next action for each material risk. Escalate unresolved blockers before launch. If an issue affects buyability, shipping, receiving or viability, record a deliberate GO / NO-GO decision.

What this prevents

Most launch problems begin as small ownership gaps.

Identifier mismatches, incomplete content, unclear PO ownership, unverified routing, missing ASN evidence and unmanaged deductions are easier to prevent than repair. This checklist makes those dependencies visible early.
